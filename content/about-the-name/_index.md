## Zhang de Re

The domain **zhangdere.com** is a small philosophy joke. Philosophers use the expressions *de re* and *de dicto* to distinguish, roughly, between claims about a thing and claims about what is said or described. Combining *Zhang* with *de re* produced a memorable domain name for a philosopher named Zhang. No substantive philosophical position should be inferred from the pun.
